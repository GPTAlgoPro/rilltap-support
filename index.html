星汐奇航玩法

这里提供《星汐奇航》的玩法帮助、设置说明与开发者支持。

拖匣接料

匣子按轮廓在棋盘上移动。把同色匣口送到料槽，接满后它就消去。

错轨双发

箭头标出移动方向。连在一起的匣子会一起走，先给它们留好退路。

反向潮汐

水下沉，油上浮。切换双仓的接料颜色，两仓分别保留进度。

星级

在目标时间内接满可得三星。超时后仍可继续，重玩只保留更好的成绩。

常见问题

怎样开始一关？

首页点“开始”进入双星口，或点“选关”选择世界和关卡。

怎样获得更高星级？

在目标时间内接满可得三星。超时后仍可继续，重玩只保留更好的成绩。

关卡和作品需要联网吗？

十个世界共100关，音乐和关卡随游戏安装，支持离线游玩。

进度保存在哪里？

作品、星级与声音设置保存在本机。当前没有游戏内账号或独立云同步服务。删除应用、重装或换设备可能影响本机进度；系统备份按你的设备设置管理。

怎样关闭音乐、音效或触感？

返回首页，进入“设置”，可分别调整声音、音乐、音效与触感开关。触感效果取决于设备支持。

为什么倾斜没有反应？

倾斜手机可以改变材料流向。设备运动仅在本机处理。

遇到卡住或异常怎么办？

先返回关卡选择，再重新进入。若仍有问题，可关闭后重新打开应用，并通过下面的邮箱反馈。请先保留现有应用，避免为排查问题而删除本机进度。

Playing Starspill Harbor

Find gameplay help, settings guidance and developer support for Starspill Harbor.

Catch and clear

Slide a tray under matching material. Fill every chamber to clear the tray.

Rail Jam

Follow the arrows. Linked trays move together, so leave room for both.

Tide Flip

Water sinks and oil floats. Switch an inlet to change which material it takes. Each chamber keeps its fill.

Stars

Fill all trays within the target time for three stars. Keep playing after the timer runs out. Replays keep your best rating.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start a stage?

Play starts at Twin Star Ports. Choose Levels to open any world and stage.

How do I earn more stars?

Fill all trays within the target time for three stars. Keep playing after the timer runs out. Replays keep your best rating.

Do the stages and creations need internet access?

Ten worlds contain 100 puzzles. Stages and audio are bundled for offline play.

Where is progress saved?

Your collection, stars and sound settings are saved on this device. There is no in-game account or separate cloud-sync service. Deleting or reinstalling the app or changing devices can affect local progress. System backups are managed through your device settings.

How do I turn off music, effects or haptics?

Open Settings from the home screen. Sound, music, sound effects and haptics have separate controls. Haptic feedback depends on device support.

Why does tilting do nothing?

Tilt the device to steer materials. Motion is processed on your device.

What should I do if something gets stuck?

Return to stage selection and reopen the stage. If needed, close and reopen the app and contact us below. Keep the current installation while troubleshooting so that local progress is preserved.
