常见问题

怎样开始一关？

首页点“开始”，或从“作品册”选择章节和关卡。点可调整的机关改变方向，查看底部出口需要的材料与数量，再点“投料”。不正确时可以调整后重试。

怎样获得更高星级？

尽量减少机关调整次数和试投失误。完成关卡会获得作品部件；重玩可以改进路线，并保留已经获得的最好星级。

关卡和作品需要联网吗？

四章共40关和作品内容随应用提供，可离线游玩。游戏不要求建立账号。

进度保存在哪里？

作品、星级与声音设置保存在本机。当前没有游戏内账号或独立云同步服务。删除应用、重装或换设备可能影响本机进度；系统备份按你的设备设置管理。

怎样关闭音乐、音效或触感？

返回首页，进入“设置”，可分别调整声音、音乐、音效与触感开关。触感效果取决于设备支持。

为什么倾斜没有反应？

主线四章使用点按机关，不需要倾斜。部分精选挑战和材料预览支持设备运动控制；这项体验需要相应设备能力，运动信息仅在本机处理。

遇到卡住或异常怎么办？

先返回关卡选择，再重新进入。若仍有问题，可关闭后重新打开应用，并通过下面的邮箱反馈。请先保留现有应用，避免为排查问题而删除本机进度。

Frequently asked questions

How do I start a stage?

Choose Play on the home screen, or choose a chapter and stage from Collection. Tap adjustable mechanisms to change direction, read the materials and amounts at the outlets, then choose Release. Adjust and retry if the result is incorrect.

How do I earn more stars?

Use fewer adjustments and avoid unsuccessful releases. Each completed stage adds a component to your collection. Replay to improve your route; your best star score is kept.

Do the stages and creations need internet access?

All four chapters, their 40 stages and the creations are included with the app and work offline. A game account is not required.

Where is progress saved?

Your collection, stars and sound settings are saved on this device. There is no in-game account or separate cloud-sync service. Deleting or reinstalling the app or changing devices can affect local progress. System backups are managed through your device settings.

How do I turn off music, effects or haptics?

Open Settings from the home screen. Sound, music, sound effects and haptics have separate controls. Haptic feedback depends on device support.

Why does tilting do nothing?

The four main chapters use tap controls and do not need tilting. Some Featured Challenges and the material preview support device-motion controls. Those modes need compatible hardware and process motion information on the device.

What should I do if something gets stuck?

Return to stage selection and reopen the stage. If needed, close and reopen the app and contact us below. Keep the current installation while troubleshooting so that local progress is preserved.
