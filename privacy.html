隐私政策

最后更新：2026年11月13日

本政策说明《拨粒 / Rilltap》如何处理信息。开发者与支持联系人为孙凯 / Kai Sun。

游戏内的数据处理

Rilltap不建立游戏账号，不接入广告、跨应用跟踪或第三方行为分析服务。当前游戏没有向开发者服务器发送个人资料、游玩记录或设备标识的功能。

本机进度与设置

作品部件、关卡星级、选择状态以及声音和触感设置保存在设备本地，用于继续游玩和保持偏好。游戏不向开发者上传这些信息。系统可能依据你的设备设置进行备份；本游戏没有独立云同步服务。

设备运动、声音与计时

部分可选材料玩法读取设备运动方向，用来控制材料流向。运动信息在设备内处理，不上传，也不用于识别你。主线四章不需要这项权限。

音乐与音效使用应用自带的音频。游戏只播放声音，不录制麦克风。动画、音频与性能计算会使用设备内的计时API；这些计时信息不用于跟踪或发送给开发者。

主动联系支持

如果你自愿发送支持邮件，我们会收到邮件内容及你主动提供的联系方式、设备或问题信息，并仅用于回复和排查该请求，不用于广告或用户画像。游戏不会自动附上设备资料或截图。

支持网站

本支持网站由GitHub Pages托管。提供网页所需的常规连接信息由GitHub处理，详见GitHub隐私声明。页面未加入广告或访问分析脚本。

你的选择

你可在游戏设置中控制声音和触感，在系统设置中管理运动权限。删除应用会移除其本机数据，系统备份的处理取决于设备设置。如果你希望询问或删除此前主动发来的支持资料，可通过下方邮箱联系。

政策更新

如果游戏的数据处理方式发生变化，本页会更新日期与说明。

Privacy Policy

Updated November13,2026

This policy explains how Rilltap / 拨粒 handles information. The developer and support contact is Kai Sun / 孙凯.

Information in the game

Rilltap does not create game accounts or integrate advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party behavioral analytics. The current game has no feature that sends personal details, gameplay records or device identifiers to a developer-operated server.

Local progress and settings

Creation components, stage stars, selections and sound/haptic preferences are stored locally so you can continue playing with your chosen settings. The game does not upload this information to the developer. Device settings may allow system backups; the game has no separate cloud-sync service.

Motion, audio and timing

Some optional material modes read device motion to steer materials. Motion information is processed on the device, is not uploaded and is not used to identify you. The four main chapters do not require this permission.

Music and effects are bundled with the app. The game plays audio and does not record the microphone. Animations, audio and performance calculations use on-device timing APIs. This timing information is not used for tracking or sent to the developer.

Support you choose to request

If you voluntarily send a support email, we receive the message and the contact, device or issue details you choose to provide. We use them to reply and investigate that request, not for advertising or profiling. The game does not automatically attach device information or screenshots.

The support website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub handles the ordinary connection information needed to serve web pages; see theGitHub Privacy Statement. These pages include no advertising or visitor-analytics scripts.

Your choices

You can control audio and haptics in the game and manage motion permission in system settings. Deleting the app removes its local data; system backups follow your device settings. Contact the email below to ask about or request deletion of information you previously sent for support.

Changes

If the game's information practices change, this page will be updated with a new date and explanation.
